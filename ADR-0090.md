## ADR-0090 — EC-0001 Stage 1 allocation and testnet readiness boundary
Status: accepted implementation clarification
Date: 2026-10-04

Problem:
Stage 1 defines a 10% founder allocation and a 20% community allocation. Genesis issuance is only 300M BOD, so the community allocation cannot be represented solely as genesis-minted balance: 150M is issued at genesis and 50M remains in the unissued community reserve.

Decision:
Keep genesis allocations as actual genesis-minted balances. Add explicit allocation_targets to the token policy so long-term program allocation and genesis issuance cannot be conflated.

The Stage 1 candidate configuration is:
- founder: 100M BOD / 10% of cap;
- community: 200M BOD / 20% of cap, consisting of 150M genesis issuance + 50M reserve;
- liquidity/market: 50M BOD / 5% of cap.

Add protocol/TESTNET_READINESS_v0.1.yml as the executable launch-surface boundary. Arbitrum Sepolia remains an adapter integration testnet only. A native BOD network is explicitly not ready and is not the current target.

Consequence:
The economic policy now represents the requested Stage 1 configuration without changing the 1B hard cap or genesis minted supply. Testnet preparation can proceed without implying a native BOD launch or final monetary policy.
